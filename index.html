About

A designer's eye, a marketer's sense of what sells, and AI agents that help do the work.

I started in graphic and web design, then spent four years at MEDNAX Medical Group keeping 12 medical-practice websites current across the country. After that I worked in marketing, grew an Instagram store to $100K+ in sales in ten months, and closed $4.7M+ in Miami real estate.

Today I design and direct every project and use Claude and ChatGPT every day to write code, content and automations faster. Clients get polished work in less time, and I can take a site from blank page to launch on my own.

I also build AI agents. My agent, Lucas, finds jobs on AI marketplaces, bids on them and delivers the work on its own: sourced research, lead lists, images, websites and AI-generated videos. Other AI agents can even hire it and pay per request. Building Lucas taught me how to give an agent the right tools, clear rules and a way to check its own work, and I bring that same approach to agents and automations for businesses.
